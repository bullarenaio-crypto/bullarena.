KNOWLEDGE BASE

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential protocol details regarding settlements, non-custodial custody, and $SOL payouts.

How does 1-click betting work on X (Twitter) and Telegram? +
Through Solana Blinks (Actions), any Bull Royale duel link shared on X (Twitter) embeds as an interactive transaction card directly in the user's timeline. Followers can back their contender with 1 click directly inside X. Similarly, our Telegram Duel Bot allows full duel participation inside groups and channels.
How does the Candle Propulsion & Buyback mechanism work? +
Every round deducts a 5% fee from the loser pool. 100% of this fee in native $SOL is programmatically used to buy back the $BULL token on decentralized exchanges and instantly burn it. Simultaneously, live market routing forces real buy volume on the token being backed, physically pumping candles on DexScreener.
What is the difference between Flash Arena (1H) and Daily Royale (24H)? +
The Flash Arena (1H) operates on fast momentum bursts and closes at the top of every hour with a 3-minute anti-snipe buffer. The Daily Royale (24H) accumulates a much larger macro liquidity pool, settling once per day at 00:00 UTC with a 15-minute buffer.
What token is used to participate during the Beta phase? +
During Phase 1 Beta, all arena showdowns operate exclusively with native Solana ($SOL). This guarantees maximum liquidity, instant payouts, and zero speculative slippage.
How does the DexScreener Oracle prevent human tampering? +
Settlements are determined programmatically using public 24-hour volume data returned by the DexScreener API at the exact second of round closure. The operators have zero backdoor access to amend numbers.
