KNOWLEDGE BASE

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential protocol details regarding settlements, non-custodial custody, and $SOL payouts.

What are the minimum and maximum wager limits? +
To prevent pool manipulation and protect payout multipliers, all wagers are strictly capped between a minimum of 0.20 SOL and a maximum of 5.00 SOL per wager during the Phase 1 Beta arena.
How does the protocol fee work? +
During the Beta Phase, a flat 5% protocol operations fee is deducted from the gross round pool to maintain autonomous non-custodial PDA vault engines, RPC feeds, and real-time oracle tracking. 95% is distributed proportionally to winning wallets.
Can I bet on the next round while the current one is closing? +
Yes! Through our Dual-Round Pipeline, when the Live Room enters its final 15 minutes (Anti-Snipe buffer), you can switch to the Next Room (Upcoming Round) and place your wagers in advance with zero interruption.
How does 1-click betting work on X (Twitter) and Telegram? +
Through Solana Blinks (Actions), any Bull Royale duel link shared on X (Twitter) embeds as an interactive transaction card directly in the user's timeline. Followers can back their contender with 1 click directly inside X. Similarly, our Telegram Duel Bot allows full duel participation inside groups and channels.
What is the difference between Flash Arena (1H) and Daily Royale (24H)? +
The Flash Arena (1H) operates on fast momentum bursts and closes at the top of every hour with a 15-minute anti-snipe buffer. The Daily Royale (24H) accumulates a much larger macro liquidity pool, settling once per day at 00:00 UTC with a 15-minute buffer.
How does the DexScreener Oracle prevent human tampering? +
Settlements are determined programmatically using public volume data returned by the DexScreener API at the exact second of round closure. The operators have zero backdoor access to amend numbers.
