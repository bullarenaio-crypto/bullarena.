FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the Bull Royale protocol, non-custodial custody, and settlements.

What tokens are required to place a stake in the Arena? +
Stakes are placed exclusively using $BULL tokens on the Solana network. A minuscule amount of SOL (less than 0.001 SOL) is needed to cover standard Solana gas fees when submitting the transaction.
Which Web3 wallets are supported on Bull Royale? +
Bull Royale natively supports Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, OKX Wallet, and MetaMask via the official Solana Snap integration.
How does the DexScreener Oracle prevent human tampering? +
The winning contender is calculated deterministically via verifiable public 24-hour volume data returned by the DexScreener API exactly at 23:59:59 UTC. The admin team has zero capability to alter numbers or override results.
Can I cancel or retrieve my stake before 23:59 UTC? +
No. Once a stake is signed and deposited into the Program Derived Address (PDA) vault, it remains locked until settlement. This prevents pool manipulation, flash-loan spoofing, and last-second unfair exits.
What happens in the rare event of a mathematical tie? +
In the improbable event of identical 24-hour volume down to the dollar, the round is declared void: 100% of all staked tokens are returned to user wallets with zero burn or fee deductions.
