LAUNCHPAD

Anti-Rug Pull & Anti-Dump Rules

Maximum purchase: 15 SOL per wallet per launch. Each wallet may complete only one purchase in that launch, even if it buys less than 15 SOL. A second purchase must be rejected.

The contract must record the first successful purchase for each wallet and launch, and reject repeat purchases atomically. Failed transactions do not count. Changing devices or reconnecting a wallet must not reset eligibility. Verification is not connected in this demo.

Maximum total supply: 500,000,000 tokens. The final supply is minted once, then mint authority must be permanently revoked. Farming cannot create additional tokens.

At the verified Raydium launch, 97% of the supply is unlocked. The creator’s 3% is the only exception: it stays locked for 2 hours after that launch, then unlocks in full. The two-hour clock does not start when this form is submitted.

With a supply of 500 million, the creator allocation is 15 million tokens; the other 485 million are unlocked at launch. Unlocking does not mean all tokens are sold or placed in liquidity.

These anti-rug pull and anti-dump rules are intended to reduce supply inflation and immediate creator selling. They do not eliminate rug pulls, price losses, or whale selling. A per-wallet cap can be bypassed with multiple wallets.

The mint restriction, creator allocation, launch timestamp, and lock must be enforced by verified on-chain contracts. This interface currently displays the policy; it does not deploy or enforce it.

How It Works

  1. Choose a side. Select Shiba or Doge.
  2. Enter an open room. Connect a wallet and review the entry terms.
  3. Follow the volume. The side with the highest verified DEX trading volume during the 30-minute round wins.

For valid rounds with bets on both sides, the total pool is split: 0.5% to the creator, 1% to the platform, and 98.5% to the winning side. Each winner receives the winning pool multiplied by their bet divided by all bets on the winning side. You can lose your bet. One-sided rounds are canceled with a 100% refund and no creator or platform fee. Blockchain fees may apply.

Zero-Fee Refund

If only one side has bets when the round ends, the round is canceled. Bettors receive 100% of their entry amount, with no protocol fee deducted. Blockchain transaction fees may still apply.

Ties and data-source failures need defined settlement rules before real-money rounds can open. These rules describe the intended protocol; on-chain settlement is not implemented in this file.
